# MATH 2040 Labs
Summary of all labs
- For each lab, students will be provided a dataset to work with
- Students will also be asked to find a realworld dataset with requirements to match the assignment
    - Students need to present a question
    - Chosen dataset needs to have variables that will work for the assignment
    - Lab is completed
    - Student makes observations and does their best to answer the presented question
